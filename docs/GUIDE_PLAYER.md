# The Hidden Covenant: Player's Guide

Welcome, Grandmaster.

You have been chosen to lead the Hidden Covenant. Your "Order" is your own life, and your mission is to maintain stability against the encroaching chaos of existence.

This guide will teach you how to wield the tools of the Covenant to survive and thrive.

---

## 1. The Core Mechanics

### Resources (Your Power)
Resources are the currency of your will. You earn them by completing tasks involved in that domain.

| Resource | Symbol | Gained By | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Influence** | 🪙 | Career, Professional Tasks | Purchasing assets, unlocking higher tiers of play. |
| **Order** | ⚖️ | Cleaning, Organizing, Admin | Directly reduces Entropy. Keep your physical space clean. |
| **Connection** | 🤝 | Socializing, Family, Friends | Directly reduces Solitude. Maintain your human bonds. |

### Threats (The Enemy)
Threats are the creeping forces of chaos. They grow automatically over time. **If ANY Threat reaches 100, your Order will disappear from existance.**

| Threat | Represents | Counter With |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Entropy** | Chaos, Mess, Decay | **Order** (Cleaning, Fixing) |
| **Stagnation** | Boredom, Lack of Progress | **Influence** (Working, Learning) |
| **Solitude** | Loneliness, Isolation | **Connection** (Calling friends, Meeting people) |

> **Warning**: Threats grow even when the game is closed (Offline Progress). Return regularly to maintain the balance.

## 2. The HUD: Your Dashboard

The game interface is divided into three main sections:

### The Sanctuary (Home)
The abstract visualization of your inner world.
-   **The Core**: The central orb represents your Will. It will get richer as you gain Influence.
-   **The Geometry**: Rings and shapes that evolve based on your Order. Higher Order will produce more and thicker rings.
-   **The Void**: The black background where stars will appear as you gain Connection.
-   **The Distortions**: Threats (Entropy, Stagnation, Solitude) manifest as visual distortions. Entropy will distort the geometry, Stagnation will slow down the geometry and freeze the screen, and Solitude will darken the whole picture.

### The Ledger (Task Manager)
Your command center. This is where you issue decrees.
-   **Draft Decree**: Button in the top-right to create new custom tasks.
-   **Operations Grid**: Your active Quests, Rituals, and Standard Protocols appear here as cards.
-   **Card Details**: Each card shows the Title, Description, and the Rewards/Penalties involved.

### The Sidebar (Navigation)
-   **The Sanctuary**: Return to the visualizer.
-   **The Ledger**: Manage your tasks.
-   **Archives**: View the history of your actions, events, and logs.
-   **Achievements**: (Trophy Icon) View your earned badges and milestones.
-   **Threats**: (Bottom Left) Real-time monitoring of Entropy, Stagnation, and Solitude levels.

## 3. Operations: Taking Action

To fight back the threats, you must execute **Operations**.

### Types of Operations
1.  **Actions**: One-off tasks. (e.g., "Wash Dishes"). Good for quick resource bursts.
2.  **Rituals**: Recurring tasks (e.g., "Daily Gym", "Weekly Review"). These have a cooldown and are better for maintenance. If you successfully complete a Ritual, you will gain resources and reduce threats, but if you fail, the rewards will become penalties.
3.  **Quests**: Special, temporary tasks. These have a deadline and offer rewards that may become penalties if not completed in time.

### Creating a Custom Decree
1.  Click **"Draft Decree"** (+) in the Ledger.
2.  **Title**: Name your task (e.g., "Finish Report").
3.  **Type**: Choose Action, Ritual, or Quest.
4.  **Aspect**: Choose which resource this task focuses on (Stability/Order, Cohesion/Connection, Ambition/Influence).
5.  **Duration/Deadline**: For Rituals (recurrence) or Quests (deadline).
6.  **Confirm**.

### Executing an Operation
1.  Perform the task in real life.
2.  Click the **Checkmark** button on the card in the Ledger.
3.  Receive your rewards immediately.

> **Warning**: Obviously, you *could* just click the checkmark without doing the task, but that would completely ruin the experience.

## 4. Events and The Narrative

Occasionally, the game will present you with a **Narrative Event**.
These are situations from your life that require attention—a cluttered desk, a forgotten text message, a feeling of stagnation.

### Making Choices
Events offer choices:
-   **Accept**: Usually grants a Quest. High reward, but requires real work.
-   **Reject/Ignore**: A quick way to dismiss the event, but often comes with a penalty (Threat increase).

### The Comeback Mechanic
If your threats are critically high (>80), you may trigger a **Relief Event**. These are "Hail Mary" opportunities to bring a threat down significantly with one decisive action.

## 5. Achievements

Your survival is worth recording. The Achievements section (accessible via the Trophy icon) tracks your triumphs.

### Categories
-   **Survival**: Enduring for 7, 30, or 100 days.
-   **Wealth**: Accumulating significant stockpiles of Resources.
-   **Risk**: Letting Threats get dangerously high and surviving (Brinkmanship).
-   **Recovery**: Coming back from the brink of defeat.
-   **Events**: Establishing a history of resolved narrative events.

Each achievement unlocks a Badge that serves as a permanent testament to your capability as Grandmaster.

---

## 6. Difficulty Levels

You can adjust the difficulty in the Settings. This controls how fast Threats grow.

-   **Easy**: For a relaxed experience. Threats grow very slowly (~1 month to failure).
-   **Medium**: The intended experience. Balanced growth (~1 week to failure).
-   **Hard**: For the disciplined. Fast growth (~3 days to failure).
