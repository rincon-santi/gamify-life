# The Hidden Covenant

## Overview
**The Hidden Covenant** is a productivity RPG where you play as the Grandmaster of an ancient, invisible order (your will). Your "Realm" is your real life, and your mission is to maintain stability against the encroaching void of **Entropy**, **Stagnation**, and **Solitude**.

It transforms mundane chores into "Operations" and tracks your personal growth through "Resources" like Influence, Order, and Connection.

## Core Philosophy
> You do not "do chores"; you execute Operations to maintain the stability of the Realm.

The game is designed to provide *immediate positive feedback* (Resource gain) and *consequential negative feedback* (Threat growth) for your real-world actions.
